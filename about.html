About Motshelo

The digital home
for Botswana's savings culture.

Millions of Batswana save together through motshelo. We built the tool that makes it work — properly, transparently, and with proof.

The Problem

What's broken.

Motshelo groups have run for generations on trust and a notebook. But the same issues keep hurting families across Botswana.

  • No record

    Nobody knows who paid and who didn't. Cash gets lost. Notebooks get damaged.

  • Disputes break groups

    "I already paid" vs "no you didn't." Groups dissolve. Friendships end.

  • No proof of savings

    When a member tries to get a loan, the bank won't accept "I'm in a stokvel."

  • Treasurer burnout

    One person holds all the stress. If they lose the book, everything falls apart.

The Solution

What we built.

Motshelo is a digital savings system designed specifically for Botswana's savings groups. No bank account required. No internet required. Works on any phone.

  • Every payment recorded

    Date, amount, method, and note. Visible to the whole group. No arguing.

  • Proof of payment uploads

    Members upload screenshots from Orange Money, MyZaka, or receipts. Treasurer approves.

  • Automatic rotation

    Whose turn is next is always clear. Marking someone as "received" moves the cycle forward.

  • Proof of savings

    Every member can generate a report showing their real savings history. Ready for banks, landlords, or anyone.

How It Works

Four simple steps.

01

Create a Group

Set the name, contribution amount, and frequency. Done in 30 seconds.

02

Add Members

Each member is added to the rotation. Their payout position is set automatically.

03

Record Payments

Log every contribution as it happens. Attach proof if you want treasurer approval.

04

Build Proof

After a few cycles, every member has a real savings report they can show anyone.
